**The Target is now a lighthouse.** The two tower blocks are gone. In their
place stands a single tapering lighthouse built of red and white blocks, with
windows, a door, a railed gallery, a glass lantern and a red cone roof. It is
round to look at and to hit. Its brick, stone and glass each take damage
differently, and Debris comes off in the colour of what was hit.
