**Buildings now collapse.** Knock out a building's lower floors and the
floors above either pancake down onto the damage or tip over toward the
damaged side. A falling building smashes into anything it lands on, damaging
the building next door. A debug control sets how easily buildings tip, and an auto mode (P) keeps
firing balls at the buildings, rebuilding them once they're all down (#12).
