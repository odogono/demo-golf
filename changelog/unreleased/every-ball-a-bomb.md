**Every ball is now a bomb.** Any ball explodes the moment it touches a
building, with the same blast however fast it was going. On anything else it
bounces and rolls like a golf ball, and your next shot is played from where it
stops, so a Hole can be worked towards over several Strokes. After an
explosion, or a ball that rolls off the course, you play again from the same
spot. The debug panel now tunes each ball's blast (#34).
