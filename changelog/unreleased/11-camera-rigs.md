**The camera now follows the Shot.** While aiming, the camera sits behind
the golfer and turns with your aim; press V to switch to a raised view
looking down the aim line. A shadow trail on the ground under the flight
preview ends in a ring where the ball will land. Once you shoot, the camera
chases the ball; at the first hit it pulls back to circle the buildings, and
time slows just before impact while big Blasts shake the camera. Press O
for a free orbit camera, and tune every camera in the debug panel (#11).
