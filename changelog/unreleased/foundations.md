**Buildings stand on Foundations.** The stone plinths under the lighthouse,
car park, glass office and arch bridge can no longer be destroyed and no
longer count towards the share Destroyed, so a building is down once what
stands on the plinth is down. Rubble rests on them and the ball rolls on them.
Rebuilding the Hole no longer resets the lighthouse's tipping (#23).
