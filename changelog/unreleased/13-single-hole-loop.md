**The Hole can now be won.** Every Shot is a Stroke against a Par of 5. Each
turn waits until the scene has Settled, or 6 seconds have passed, and anything
still moving then freezes into Rubble. Once the lighthouse's Height falls below
30% of where it started, a card shows the score against Par and offers to play
again (R). Par, the win line and the settle timeout are tunable in the debug
panel.
