**Ornaments and sideways curves for buildings.** Blueprints can now curve a
Cell around a horizontal axis, such as the underside of an arch, and the ball
hits the curve as drawn. They can also add Ornaments: details like lamp posts
and railings that are drawn on a Cell but have no weight or collision. An
Ornament falls with its Cell and is gone once the Cell is destroyed (#45).
